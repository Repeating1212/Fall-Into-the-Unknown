Introduction to level 1
- Purpose: Teach player for "Move-Attack-Gain reward-Upgrade-Challenge harder"
- How to implement each steps

Explain the player work flow:
1. Player get trapped ("Use slash")
2. Player move toward chest
3. Player attack chest, chest health bar reduce
4. Player attack chest, chest drop item
5. Player upgrade (Increase damage)
6. Guard (Boss) wake up and fight
7. Player fight against it
8. Chest drop
9. New ability (Protect)
