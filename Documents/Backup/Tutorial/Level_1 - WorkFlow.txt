Introduce to Damage Mechanic

Content Table:
- Initialize game
- Gameplay flow
- Explanation

1. Slash mechanic
- At start, the player is trapped (Unable to move)
- Player need to hit the trap (Use slash skill)
- Player released, now able to move

2. Move mechanic
- A shiny box is put in the center
- A boss health bar with label "Treasure Box"
- Player need to move to the "Treasure Box"

3. Cooldown Mechanic and Damage Machinic
- Treasure has three heart
- Slash skill deal two damage
- Thus:
-- Player need to damage two time the "Treasure Box"
-- The "Treasure Box" health bar will reduce
-- The skill will enter cooldown

4. Levels and Coin Mechanic
-- Player get coin and experiences
-- Player will level up
-- Player get new skill (Protect)
-- A notification will pop out
-- Upgrade Slash skill so that "Treasure Box" can be one-hit KO

5. Challenge Harder Mechanic
-- Boss (Guard) wake up
-- Player need to attack the boss
-- Boss stunned after it attack
-- Player use the time to attack it
-- Boss exit from stunned enter rigid state
-- Player slash in cooldown
-- Player need to attack twice to complete the boss
-- A chest drop
-- The chest now can be one-hit KO (Player increased damage)
-- New ability (Protect)
